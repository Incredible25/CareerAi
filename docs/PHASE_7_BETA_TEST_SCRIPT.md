# CareerAI Beta — Participant Guide

Thank you for trying CareerAI. This guide walks you through everything to
do. There's no right or wrong outcome we're hoping for — we want your
honest reaction, including if something feels off, unhelpful, or wrong.
Negative feedback is exactly as useful to us as positive feedback, so
please don't hold back either way.

## What to do

1. **Create an account.** Use your real email and answer the questions
   honestly, including your age range — this affects what the app shows
   you, and we need that to work correctly.

2. **Complete your profile.** Fill in your education, skills, interests,
   and goals as they actually are — not as you think would produce a
   better result. The recommendations are only as good as what you tell
   us, and we're testing the real thing, not a best-case scenario.

3. **Complete the assessment honestly.** Answer each question about how
   you actually think and work, not how you'd like to be seen. There's
   no passing or failing score.

4. **Review your recommended careers.** Look at the list you're given —
   the career names, the fit scores, and how they're ranked.

5. **Read the explanations.** Each recommendation shows why it was
   suggested. Actually read them, not just the score.

6. **Identify which recommendations feel relevant.** For each one that
   feels like a genuinely good fit, note *why* it feels that way — was it
   the explanation, something you already knew about yourself, both?

7. **Identify which recommendations feel irrelevant.** This step matters
   as much as step 6. For each one that doesn't feel right, note that too
   — don't skip past it or assume you're missing something. If it feels
   wrong, it might be wrong.

8. **Explain why, in your own words**, for both the relevant and
   irrelevant ones. "It just doesn't feel right" is a completely valid
   answer — you don't need a technical reason.

9. **Try it on your normal device and network**, not a borrowed laptop
   or someone else's fast wifi. If your normal experience is a shared
   phone with patchy data, use that. That's exactly the condition we
   need to see.

10. **Submit feedback on each recommendation** using the thumbs up/down
    and the reason options shown. Pick whichever reason actually matches
    what you felt, even if it's "I want more information" or "Other" —
    there's a text box for anything the listed reasons don't capture.

## A few things worth knowing

- **You can stop and come back.** Nothing is timed, and your progress is
  saved as you go.
- **There's no "correct" set of recommendations.** We're testing whether
  the tool understands people like you, not whether you can get it to
  produce a specific answer.
- **If something breaks, confuses you, or feels wrong in a way the
  thumbs-up/down doesn't capture**, tell us that directly — however you
  were told to reach the team running this beta. That kind of report is
  just as valuable as the structured feedback.
- **Take as long as you need.** A rushed answer to "is this relevant"
  isn't as useful to us as a considered one.

That's it — thank you again for helping test this honestly.
